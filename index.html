You'll take a front and a side photo in a fitted top or unpadded bra. You need to be 18 or over to use this.

Your photos are processed on this device and are never uploaded or saved.

Before you start

  1. Wear an unpadded, unlined bra or a thin fitted top. Padding and loose tops throw off the measurement.
  2. Tie long hair up and take your shoes off.
  3. Prop your phone upright at chest height, about 2 m away, so you're in the photo from the top of your head to at least your knees (head to feet is slightly more accurate). A plain wall behind you helps.
  4. Front photo: face the camera, feet together, arms held out about 45° from your sides.
  5. Side photo: turn sideways, arms relaxed by your sides. Stand naturally and breathe normally.

Check the fit

  • The band sits level and snug on the loosest hook.
  • No gaping or spilling at the top of the cup.
  • The centre of the underwire lies flat against your breastbone.
  • Underwires sit behind the breast tissue at the sides, not on it.

Sizes vary between brands, so treat this as a starting point. Nothing has been saved.
